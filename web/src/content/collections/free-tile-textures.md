Tile materials cover bathrooms, kitchens, floors, pools and facades: glazed ceramic squares, subway tiles, hexagons, mosaics, terracotta, checkerboards and patterned cement tiles. Because they are geometric, tile textures are some of the easiest materials to tile without visible seams.

This page brings together free tile materials from twelve texture sites with one live search, refreshed automatically: Poly Haven, ambientCG, CGBookcase, TextureCan, ShareTextures, 3DTextures.me and others. Each card shows its source and licence; most are CC0.

## What to check

- **Grout depth.** The normal and displacement maps should push the grout lines back so they catch shadow. That is what makes a tiled wall look built rather than printed.
- **Glaze roughness.** Glazed ceramic is glossy and grout is rough. A good roughness map separates the two, so highlights stop at each tile's edge.
- **Variation.** Hand-made tiles differ slightly in colour and flatness. Materials that capture this look far more believable than perfectly uniform ones.

Combine tiles with marble and wood from the related collections to dress kitchens and bathrooms quickly.
