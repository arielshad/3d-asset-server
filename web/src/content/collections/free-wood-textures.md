Wood covers more ground in 3D scenes than almost any other material: floors, decking, furniture, cabinets, crates, fences and the bark of trees. Free wood materials range from clean veneers and parquet to weathered planks and rough bark, and most libraries offer them as complete PBR sets.

This page brings together wood materials from twelve texture sites with one live search, refreshed automatically: Poly Haven, ambientCG, CGBookcase, TextureCan, ShareTextures, 3DTextures.me and others. Each card shows its source and licence.

## Picking the right wood

- **Planks and floors** come as tileable boards with gaps between them. Check the plank width in the preview against your room's scale.
- **Raw timber and veneers** suit furniture. A material with a long, unbroken grain avoids visible repeats on table tops.
- **Weathered wood** (grey, cracked, painted and peeling) suits fences, barns and old crates.
- **Bark** is its own category: high-contrast, deeply displaced and best used with a displacement or parallax map.

For real-time use, 2K colour, normal and roughness maps are enough. For close-up renders of furniture, 4K keeps the grain sharp.
