Medieval assets are the building blocks of fantasy and historical games: castles and towers, timber-framed village houses, market stalls, carts, barrels, swords and shields, banners and furniture. They work for RPGs, strategy games, city builders and tabletop-style scenes.

This page gathers free medieval models and packs from several sites with one live search, refreshed automatically: Quaternius, Polyfork, BlenderKit, Poly Haven, ShareTextures and itch.io creators among them. Each card shows its licence and formats.

## What to look for

- **Modularity.** Wall, roof and floor pieces that snap together let you build any number of houses and castles from one kit.
- **Props.** Barrels, crates, carts, fences and market goods make a village feel lived in. Check whether a pack includes them or only buildings.
- **Style match.** Low-poly kits with flat colours fit together easily; scanned or realistic props need realistic buildings around them.
- **Characters.** For a playable scene, combine the environment with animated characters from the related collection.

Brick, wood and rock materials from the texture collections fill in walls, floors and terrain that the kits do not cover.
