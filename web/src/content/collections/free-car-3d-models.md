Car models are needed everywhere: racing and driving games, street scenes, architectural renders, product shots and traffic in city levels. Free cars range from detailed render-quality models to low-poly game cars with a few hundred triangles.

This page gathers free car and vehicle models from several sites with one live search, refreshed automatically: BlenderKit, Poly Haven, 3DTexel, Kenney, Quaternius and itch.io creators among them. Each card shows its licence and formats.

## What to check

- **Separate parts.** For driving, you need wheels as separate objects with their pivots at the hub. For static scenes, a single mesh is fine.
- **Polygon count.** Detailed cars can carry hundreds of thousands of triangles; a game may need a lighter model, or several levels of detail.
- **Materials.** Car paint needs a clear-coat layer, glass needs transparency and chrome needs an environment to reflect. Light cars with an HDRI so they look like cars rather than plastic toys.
- **Trademarks.** A free licence for the mesh does not grant rights to a car maker's brand.

Low-poly car packs suit mobile games and web demos; detailed models suit renders and showcases.
