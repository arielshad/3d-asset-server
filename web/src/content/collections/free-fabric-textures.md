Fabric materials dress furniture, clothing, curtains, carpets and props: cotton, linen, denim, wool, knitted cloth, velvet, leather and patterned upholstery. A good fabric material captures the weave in its normal map and the soft, matte look of cloth in its roughness.

This page collects free fabric and cloth materials from twelve texture sites with one live search, refreshed automatically: Poly Haven, ambientCG, CGBookcase, TextureCan, ShareTextures, 3DTextures.me and others. Each card shows its source and licence; most are CC0.

## Picking fabric

- **Weave scale.** Fabric detail is tiny. Check how large one tile is in the real world, or a sofa can end up looking like it is made of rope.
- **Plain or patterned.** Plain weaves can be recoloured by tinting the colour map; printed patterns cannot without editing the texture.
- **Pile and fuzz.** Velvet, wool and towels need sheen, and up close a fuzz or fibre effect, beyond what a flat texture gives.
