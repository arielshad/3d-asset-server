Food models dress kitchens, restaurants, markets and dining tables in renders, and fill inventories and shops in games: fruit, vegetables, bread, cakes, drinks, cheese and packaged goods. A few well-placed food props make an interior render feel lived in.

This page collects free food models from several sites with one live search, refreshed automatically: photoscanned food from Poly Haven and ShareTextures, models from BlenderKit, and low-poly packs from Kenney, Quaternius and itch.io creators. Each card shows its licence and formats.

## Choosing food props

- **Scanned or stylised.** Photoscanned food looks real in close-ups but carries detailed textures; low-poly food suits games, icons and web scenes.
- **Scale.** Small props are easy to get wrong. An apple is about 8 cm across; check the size before filling a table.
- **Sets.** Packs with plates, bowls and cutlery as well as food save time on table scenes.

For a food product shot, combine a scanned model with a marble or wood surface and a studio HDRI.
