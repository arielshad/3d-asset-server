Low-poly trees are the quickest way to fill a stylised world: flat-shaded pines, round oaks, palm trees, birches and autumn trees with a handful of polygons each. They suit mobile games, web scenes, prototypes and anything rendered in real time where a scanned tree with millions of leaves would be far too heavy.

This page gathers free low-poly tree models from several sites with one live search, refreshed automatically: Quaternius and Polyfork packs, BlenderKit models and itch.io asset creators among them. Each card shows its licence and formats; check the licence before shipping, because itch.io creators set their own terms.

## What to look for

- **Matching style.** Trees from one pack share colours and level of detail. Mixing packs is fine, but keep the shading style (flat or smooth, textured or vertex-coloured) consistent.
- **Variations.** A pack with several sizes and shapes of the same tree breaks up repetition in a forest.
- **Pivot and scale.** Check that the pivot sits at the base of the trunk and that the scale is in metres, so trees drop onto terrain without adjustment.

Combine them with low-poly rocks and plants from the related collections for a complete nature kit.
