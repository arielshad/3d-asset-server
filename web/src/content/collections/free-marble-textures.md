## What matters for marble

- **Vein scale.** Real marble veins run across a whole slab. A texture that tiles every 50 cm looks fake on a large floor; prefer high-resolution, wide-coverage maps.
- **Roughness.** Most of the realism comes from smooth, reflective stone with subtle variation where it has been worn.
- **Slabs and tiles.** For floors, choose between continuous slabs and tiled marble with grout lines (see the tile collection).

For product renders, a dark polished marble surface under a studio HDRI is a classic backdrop.
