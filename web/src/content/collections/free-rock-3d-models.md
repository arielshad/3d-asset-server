Rocks are the most reused props in outdoor scenes: boulders on a hillside, stones along a path, cliffs at the edge of a level, rubble in a ruin. Free rock models range from photoscanned stones with 4K and 8K textures to simple low-poly rocks with flat colours.

This page brings together free rock models from several sites with one live search, refreshed automatically: Poly Haven, Polyfork, BlenderKit, ShareTextures, 3DTexel and itch.io creators among them. Each card shows its licence and formats; most scanned rocks are CC0.

## Choosing rocks

- **Realistic or stylised.** Pick one style for the whole scene. Scanned rocks next to flat-shaded trees look out of place.
- **Flat base.** Rocks that are flat underneath sit on the ground without floating edges. Scanned rocks often have a cut-off base for this reason.
- **Sets.** A pack with several sizes, from pebbles to boulders, gives a natural spread.
- **Polygon budget.** Scanned rocks are often detailed; for real time, use their lower-resolution versions or a decimated copy.

Pair rocks with matching ground materials and plants for a complete terrain set.
