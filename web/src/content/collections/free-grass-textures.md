Grass textures cover lawns, meadows, sports fields and the ground of open-world levels. On their own they make a convincing surface from above or at a distance; combined with scattered 3D grass they form the base layer of most outdoor scenes.

## Choosing grass

- **Trimmed lawn or wild meadow.** Short, even grass tiles well; long or patchy grass looks more natural but repeats more visibly.
- **Dry, green or mixed.** Mix two grass materials with a noise mask to avoid a single flat colour across a field.
- **With dirt.** Materials that mix grass and soil help on paths and worn areas.

Tile grass at a realistic scale: one tile usually covers one to two metres. Repetition is the biggest giveaway on large terrains, so blend in a second material or vary the colour with a large noise texture.
