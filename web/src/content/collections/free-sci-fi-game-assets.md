Sci-fi game assets cover space stations, corridors and modular walls, spaceship parts, robots and mechs, futuristic weapons, crates, consoles and terminals. They suit shooters, space games, puzzle games and cyberpunk scenes.

This page collects free sci-fi models and packs from several sources with one live search, refreshed automatically: Quaternius and Kenney packs, BlenderKit models and itch.io creators among them. Each card shows its licence and formats.

## What to look for

- **Modular kits.** Corridor and room kits whose walls, floors and doors snap together on a grid let you build whole levels from a few pieces. Check the grid size in the description.
- **Emissive details.** Lights, screens and glowing trims are what make sci-fi read as sci-fi. Good assets keep them in a separate emissive material or map.
- **Matching characters.** If you need characters too, look for packs that include robots or soldiers in the same style.
- **Polygon budget.** Low-poly kits run in a browser and on mobile; detailed hard-surface models suit renders and PC games.

Pair these assets with metal and concrete materials and a dark HDRI or night sky for the background.
