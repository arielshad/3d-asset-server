Furniture models dress interiors for architectural renders, product scenes and games: chairs, armchairs, sofas, tables, beds, cabinets, shelves and lamps. Free furniture ranges from photoscanned antiques and modern designer pieces to simple low-poly props for game levels.

This page collects free furniture from several libraries with one live search, refreshed automatically, including Poly Haven, BlenderKit, ShareTextures, Kenney, Quaternius and itch.io creators. Each card shows the licence and available formats.

## Choosing furniture

- **Render quality or game-ready.** High-detail models with 4K textures look great in still renders but can be heavy in real time. Low-poly furniture packs keep the style consistent across a whole game level.
- **Real-world scale.** Good furniture models are modelled in metres. A chair seat should sit around 45 cm high; check that it does before placing a room full of them.
- **Formats.** glTF/GLB is the most portable format for the web and game engines, Blend files keep Blender materials intact, and FBX suits Unity and Unreal.

For a quick interior, pair these models with wood and fabric materials and light the room with an HDRI.
