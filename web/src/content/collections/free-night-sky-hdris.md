Night HDRIs cover everything after sunset: deep blue twilight, moonlit landscapes, starry skies and streets lit by lamps and shop windows. They set the mood of a night scene and give reflective surfaces something to reflect, but they rarely light a subject on their own.

This page gathers free night HDRIs from Poly Haven, ambientCG, BlenderKit and other libraries with one live search, refreshed automatically. Each card shows the licence, and most are CC0.

## Working with night light

- **Expect low light.** A night panorama holds far less energy than a daylight one. Turn up the exposure, or use the HDRI only for reflections and background and light the subject yourself.
- **Point lights in the image.** HDRIs taken in cities contain bright street lamps and windows. They appear as small, sharp highlights on cars, wet ground and glass, which sells a night scene.
- **Stars need resolution.** Star fields blur into noise at low resolution. If the sky is visible, use 8K or more, or replace it with a separate star map.

In a game engine, a night HDRI works well as the sky and reflection source while dynamic lights carry the gameplay lighting.
