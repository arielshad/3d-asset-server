A forest HDRI is a panorama taken among trees: patches of sky through the canopy, dappled sunlight on the ground and a lot of green light bouncing off leaves. It grounds outdoor scenes, vehicles, characters and props in a natural setting with one image.

This page collects free forest and woodland HDRIs from Poly Haven, ambientCG, BlenderKit and other libraries with a live search, refreshed automatically. Each card shows its licence; most are CC0.

## How to choose one

- **Clearing or deep forest.** A clearing lets more sky in and gives brighter, more directional light. Deep forest gives dim, soft and very green light.
- **Sun through leaves.** Some panoramas catch the sun through gaps in the canopy, which produces sharp highlights and flickering shadows on shiny surfaces.
- **Season.** Summer forests are saturated green, autumn ones warm and orange, winter ones grey and open.

Pair a forest HDRI with matching models (trees, rocks and plants from the related collections) so near objects and the background agree on lighting and colour.
