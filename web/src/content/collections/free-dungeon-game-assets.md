Dungeon assets are the classic starting point for an RPG or a roguelike: stone walls and floors, arches, doors and gates, stairs, torches, chests, barrels, bones and traps. Most come as modular kits that snap together on a grid.

This page collects free dungeon models and packs from several sites with one live search, refreshed automatically: Polyfork, Kenney, Quaternius, BlenderKit and itch.io creators among them. Each card shows its licence and formats.

## What to check

- **Grid size.** Every piece in a kit should share one grid. Mixing kits with different grid sizes leaves gaps; stick to one kit for walls and floors and mix only props.
- **Pieces included.** A usable kit has straight walls, corners, doorways, floors and ideally stairs and pillars. Packs with only a few pieces are best for decoration.
- **Props and dressing.** Chests, barrels, torches and debris turn empty rooms into a place worth exploring.
- **Performance.** Dungeons repeat the same pieces many times; combine them into batches or use instancing to keep draw calls low.
