Concrete is the backbone of urban and industrial scenes: walls, floors, pavements, parking garages, bunkers and brutalist interiors. Free concrete materials cover smooth cast concrete, board-formed walls, polished floors, cracked slabs and weathered surfaces with stains and leaks.

The materials on this page are gathered from twelve texture sites with one live search and refreshed automatically, including Poly Haven, ambientCG, TextureCan, ShareTextures, CGBookcase and 3DTextures.me. Each card shows its source and licence; most are CC0.

## What separates good concrete

- **Roughness variation.** Real concrete is rarely uniform; patches worn smooth and damp spots should show up in the roughness map.
- **Pores and pitting.** Small air holes come through in the normal and displacement maps and catch light at grazing angles.
- **Large-scale variation.** Stains, formwork marks and colour shifts make the difference between a believable wall and a flat grey plane.

For sci-fi corridors and industrial levels, combine concrete with metal trims; for streets, mix it with asphalt and tile materials.
