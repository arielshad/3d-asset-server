Plants bring life to both interiors and outdoor scenes: potted house plants on a shelf, ferns and bushes in a garden, flowers in a meadow, shrubs along a path. A few plants in a room render make it feel lived in; scattered bushes and ferns make a landscape feel natural.

This page gathers free plant models from several libraries with one live search, refreshed automatically. Poly Haven and BlenderKit supply detailed, often scanned plants; Polyfork and itch.io creators add stylised and low-poly ones. Each card shows its licence and formats.

## What to check

- **Leaf transparency.** Most plants use leaf cards with alpha textures. Your engine or renderer needs alpha clipping and double-sided rendering for them.
- **Pot or no pot.** Interior plants often come in a pot; outdoor plants come bare for scattering on terrain.
- **Detail level.** Close-up product and interior renders need detailed plants; games and web scenes need light ones.
- **Wind.** Some game-ready plants are built for wind animation, with vertex colours or separate branches. Check the description if foliage should sway.

Light plants with an HDRI so light shines through the leaves. It is what makes foliage look alive.
