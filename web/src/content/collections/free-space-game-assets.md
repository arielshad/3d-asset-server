Space game assets cover everything from arcade shooters to space sims: fighters and cargo ships, space stations, asteroids, planets and moons, satellites, astronauts and rovers. Many are low-poly and stylised, which suits the clean look of space scenes and keeps large fleets cheap to render.

This page gathers free space models and packs from several sites with one live search, refreshed automatically: Polyfork, Kenney, Quaternius, BlenderKit, Poly Haven and itch.io creators among them. Each card shows its licence and formats.

## What to look for

- **Ship sets.** A pack with several ships in one style (fighters, bombers, freighters) is worth more than one detailed ship.
- **Separate parts.** Engines, turrets and landing gear as separate objects make animation and destruction effects easier.
- **Asteroids and planets.** Rocks in several sizes and shapes, plus planets with their own textures, fill out the space between ships.
- **Lighting.** Space has one sun and no air. A single hard directional light with dark shadows looks right; soft fill light looks wrong.

Add sci-fi interiors and metal materials from the related collections to build stations and hangars.
